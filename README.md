### ALGO-000163: Polynomial diameter of polytopes
- **Subfield:** Linear optimization and enumeration
- **Open:** Prove a polynomial upper bound in dimension and number of facets on the graph diameter of every polytope.
- **Status:** The linear Hirsch conjecture is false, but its polynomial replacement remains open. Quasipolynomial upper bounds are known.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would bound the number of edge moves needed to connect feasible LP vertices.
- **Key works:** Kalai and Kleitman (1992), *A Quasi-Polynomial Bound for the Diameter of Graphs of Polyhedra*; Santos (2012), *A Counterexample to the Hirsch Conjecture*.
- **Related:** Polyhedral combinatorics—short paths on polytope graphs.
